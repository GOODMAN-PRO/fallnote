# Fallnote

## Register

product

## Users

A piano learner converting falling-note tutorial videos on a Windows PC, then downloading MIDI files to import into a VR piano app.

## Product Purpose

Import a video file or a YouTube tutorial on the PC, align a note detector with the visible keyboard, convert the coloured bars to MIDI, and automatically publish the result to a GitHub Pages library for VR downloads. Published downloads stay available with the PC switched off.

## Design Direction

A clear music workbench. The learner compares a dark video frame with calibration controls in ordinary room light. Keep the surrounding interface light, readable, and quiet. Use a restrained indigo accent for active controls and primary actions.

## Design Principles

- Put the source video and its calibration together.
- Explain what the detector sees before conversion.
- Distinguish saved local songs from the published library.
- Confirm the public download is live before calling a conversion ready in VR.
- Label algorithmic hand splitting and timing limits honestly.
- Keep downloads available without login on the public library.

## Accessibility

Use semantic controls, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion, text descriptions of colour roles, and responsive layouts.
